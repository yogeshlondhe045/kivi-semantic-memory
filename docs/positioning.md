# Product positioning statement

Kivi's semantic memory is a ledger of what you said — not a model of who you are.

It keeps what you stated or repeated, stores the sentence that proves it, and shows you both. It never infers mood, health, politics, or how you are doing. When your history does not hold an answer, Hey Kivi says so rather than producing one.

The restraint is the product. A voice interface hears everything; the reason to hand one your working day is that it demonstrably keeps almost none of it, uses what it keeps in the open, and forgets on request — permanently.
